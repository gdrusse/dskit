## Proposed decision

Create `U_r` as a finite set of joint net-return scenarios for every holding
the MIO may select. Resample entire sessions and retain each timestamp's
cross-asset vector so covariance, common shocks, and tails are not broken.
Normalize or separate incompatible holding horizons before they enter one
scenario. Record scenario weights, block rule, seed, calibration window, costs,
and source hashes.

Use `U_r` for drawdown, CVaR, gross/net exposure, and fractional-Kelly risk
constraints. It is distinct from `U_mu` (uncertain expected alpha) and
`U_pi` (uncertain false-signal probabilities).

## Research backing

Stationary/block bootstrap supports dependent resampling. The scenario
approach supplies probabilistic robustness results for sampled uncertain
constraints:

- Politis and Romano (1994), *The Stationary Bootstrap*:
  https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870
- Calafiore and Campi (2006), *The Scenario Approach to Robust Control Design*:
  https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2006.875041
- Chernozhukov, Wüthrich, and Zhu (2018), dependent conformal inference:
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v75/chernozhukov18a.html

**Decision criterion: empirical.** Prospective coverage, tail loss, and
constraint-violation frequency can validate the scenario generator.
