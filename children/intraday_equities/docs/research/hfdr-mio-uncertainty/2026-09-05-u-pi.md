## Proposed decision

Create a joint uncertainty set `U_pi` over the vector of false-signal
probabilities for all simultaneously eligible holdings. Construct candidate
vectors by refitting the empirical-Bayes calibrator on whole-session,
cross-asset resamples. The set must retain cross-name and cross-horizon
dependence and carry calibration/model/null hashes.

The first tractable form may be either a rectangular upper-bound set or a
finite scenario set. Compare realized HFDR coverage and capital efficiency
before selecting the geometry. The MIO applies the ADR-0088 constraint for
every retained scenario (or its exact robust counterpart).

## Research backing and judgement

Empirical-Bayes local FDR supports the probability inputs; robust optimization
supports optimization over uncertain coefficient sets:

- Efron (2007), *Size, Power and False Discovery Rates*:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.2245
- Ben-Tal and Nemirovski (1999), *Robust solutions of uncertain linear
  programs*: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-6377(99)00016-4

**Decision criterion: judgemental.** Joint treatment is principled, but box,
scenario, ellipsoidal, or distributional geometry is a design choice until
comparative calibration and tractability evidence exists.
