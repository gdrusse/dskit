\begin{abstract}
This document states the mixed-integer program that sizes every trading
decision in \code{configs/run-retrain-simulation.json} (ADR-0185), and how
that program is placed inside the per-tick simulation. Each equation cites
the class and method that implements it. The generic program lives in
\code{dskit/pipeline/libs/pyomo.py} (\code{ScenarioUtilitySolve}). The equity
subclass lives in
\code{children/intraday_equities/intraday_equities/nodes_capital.py}
(\code{EquityKellyMIO}). The per-tick driver lives in
\code{children/intraday_equities/intraday_equities/simulation.py}
(\code{MioDecider}). Section~\ref{sec:values} lists the exact parameter
values the backtest uses. The MIO risk values are the owner-delegated
growth policy v1: explicit judgement calls for this developmental run, not
tuned optima or deployment-validated limits.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and runtime placement}\label{sec:scope}

The staged document runs these stages in order:
\code{calendar} $\to$ \code{memory} $\to$ \code{hpo_document}, \code{hpo}
(warmup search) $\to$ \code{winners} $\to$ \code{walk_document},
\code{walk} (20 frozen-winner releases) $\to$ \code{inventory} $\to$
\code{gates} $\to$ \code{simulate}. The optimizer runs only inside
\code{simulate}, and it runs through this chain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{RetrainedSimulation.run} binds the template
  \code{configs/run-retrain-simulation-template.json} to this run's
  inventory and gates, then executes it with \code{run_document}.
\item Inside that graph, \code{ForecastPublisher} (node \code{publish}) emits
  the \code{releases} (one per fold $k=2,\dots,19$) and the \code{bundles}.
  There is one \code{ForecastBundle} per (30-minute lattice tick, lead group).
\item \code{MioDeciderNode} (node \code{decide}) validates the MIO
  parameters. It sizes nothing.
\item \code{DevelopmentSimulation.run} (node \code{simulate}) replays one
  \code{EquityReplay} segment per release. At every tick it calls
  \code{MioDecider.decide}, and that call solves one \code{EquityKellyMIO}
  per lead group (Section~\ref{sec:integration}).
\end{enumerate}
The optimizer decides \emph{target positions}. Fills, forced exits, fees and
cash are applied by \code{EquityReplay}, outside the optimizer.

\paragraph{Scenario returns.}
\impl{\code{ForecastBundle._recentered_scenarios}}
With $\tilde r_{io}=\operatorname{expm1}\big((\hat y_i+e_{io})\sigma_i\sqrt{L}\big)$,
the scenario gross returns are recentered so their weighted mean equals the
false-signal-haircut mean:
\begin{equation}
r_{io}=\tilde r_{io}-\sum_{o'}\omega_{o'}\tilde r_{io'}+(1-\hat\pi_i)\,\mu_i,
\qquad r_{io}>-1 .\label{eq:recenter}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Per-share costs.} These come from the same fill-policy knobs the
replay bills with.
\impl{\code{SchwabCostModel.buy_per_share / sell_per_share}}
\begin{equation}
\kappa^{b}_i=\beta_s p_i,\qquad
\kappa^{s}_i=\beta_s p_i+\tau+\beta_{31}p_i,
\label{eq:costs}
\end{equation}
where $\beta_s=\text{\code{spread_bps}}\cdot10^{-4}$,
$\beta_{31}=\text{\code{sec31_bps}}\cdot10^{-4}$ and
$\tau=\text{\code{taf_per_share}}$ (USD per share, uncapped).
The exit cost per share at the horizon is $\kappa^{x}_i=\kappa^{s}_i$, priced
at $p_i$.

\section{Constraints}

\subsection{Inventory, direction and eligibility}
\impl{\code{ScenarioUtilitySolve.build_model}}
\begin{align}
& Q_i \ge 0 && \text{(\code{nonneg_q}, long only)}\\
& b_i \le U_i\,d_i,\quad U_i=\lfloor \bar x_i/p_i\rfloor+h_i+1 && \text{(\code{buy_only})}\\
& s_i \le h_i\,(1-d_i) && \text{(\code{sell_only}: no same-tick buy and sell)}\\
& x_i \le \bar x_i\, y_i && \text{(\code{elig_hi}: position cap, gates } y_i)\\
& x_i \ge m\, d_i && \text{(\code{elig_lo}: minimum ticket, binds only on a buy)}\\
& \textstyle\sum_i y_i \le M && \text{(\code{cardinality})}
\end{align}
Eligibility is decided \emph{before} the program is built
(\code{EquityKellyMIO.instruments}). A row enters $\N$ only if it passes
every one of these checks:
\begin{itemize}
\item it is a survivor;
\item its capped horizon exists, is at least 1 and is at least $L$;
\item its age $t_{\text{portfolio}}-t_{\text{bundle}}$ is between 0 and
  \code{bundle_max_staleness_ms};
\item $p_i\ge$ \code{min_price}.
\end{itemize}
A held name with no surviving row enters as a mandatory exit
($\bar x_i=0$, $\tilde\pi_i=0$, $r_{io}=0$).

\subsection{Cash, reserve and buying power}
\impl{\code{ScenarioUtilitySolve.build_model: cash_after, cash_floor, buying_power}}
\begin{align}
c^{+} &= c_0+\textstyle\sum_i\big[(p_i-\kappa^s_i)s_i-(p_i+\kappa^b_i)b_i\big]\\
c^{+} &\ge R\\
\textstyle\sum_i (p_i+\kappa^b_i)\,b_i &\le B_0+\theta\sum_i (p_i-\kappa^s_i)\,s_i
\end{align}
The sale credit $\theta$ is the fraction of this tick's sale proceeds that
counts as buying power in the same tick.

\subsection{Gross exposure}
\begin{equation}
\textstyle\sum_i x_i \le G\qquad\text{(only when } G \text{ is given; \code{gross_exposure})}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Scenario terminal wealth}
\impl{\code{_wealth_rule}}
\begin{equation}
W_o = c^{+}+\sum_i Q_i\big(p_i(1+r_{io})-\kappa^{x}_i\big),\qquad o\in\Om .
\end{equation}
Entry cost enters through $c^{+}$, and exit cost through $\kappa^{x}_i$.

\subsection{Tangent-plane utility}
\impl{\code{tangent_utility}; \code{model.tangent}}
The concave utility is taken relative to $W^0$:
\begin{equation}
u(w)=\begin{cases}\ln(w/W^0), & \gamma\le 1,\\[2pt]
\dfrac{(w/W^0)^{1-\gamma}-1}{1-\gamma}, & \gamma>1,\end{cases}
\qquad u'(w)=\begin{cases}1/w,&\gamma\le1\\ (w/W^0)^{-\gamma}/W^0,&\gamma>1.\end{cases}
\end{equation}
The knots $k_j$ are $K$ equally spaced points on $[\underline W,\overline W]$. Each
scenario's utility is bounded by every tangent:
\begin{equation}
t_o \le u(k_j)+u'(k_j)\,(W_o-k_j),\qquad o\in\Om,\ j\in\mathcal J .
\end{equation}
The wealth envelope is \impl{\code{EquityKellyMIO.instruments}}
\begin{align*}
N^{\text{cap}}&=\min\big(\textstyle\sum_i \bar x_i,\ G\big),\\
\text{span}&=1.5\cdot\max\Big(N^{\text{cap}}\max\big(|\min r|,|\max r|,0.05\big),\ 0.05\max(W^0,1)\Big),\\
\underline W&=\max(0.01\,W^0,\ W^0-\text{span}),\qquad \overline W=W^0+\text{span}.
\end{align*}
Because $W_o$ is bounded to $[\underline W,\overline W]$, the envelope is
also a constraint.

\subsection{CVaR (Rockafellar--Uryasev)}
\impl{\code{model.cvar_row, model.cvar_cap}}
The loss in scenario $o$ is $\ell_o=W^0-W_o$.
\begin{align}
z_o &\ge \ell_o-\eta,\quad z_o\ge 0 \\
\eta+\tfrac{1}{1-\alpha}\textstyle\sum_o\omega_o z_o &\le C \qquad\text{(only when \code{cvar_limit} is not null)}
\end{align}

\subsection{HFDR row}
\impl{\code{EquityKellyMIO.domain_constraints: model.hfdr}}
\begin{equation}
\sum_{i\in\N}\big(\tilde\pi_i-q\big)\,x_i \le 0 .
\end{equation}
$\tilde\pi_i$ is \code{pi_widened}
(\code{HFDR_COEFFICIENT_FIELD}). It is a \emph{widened point estimate}. It is
not an upper bound, and this row is \emph{not} a chance constraint: ADR-0152
measured 0.53--0.82 attainment against a 0.95 nominal and withdrew the
\code{pi_upper} bound. The attestation records the measured floor 0.53 as a
synthetic value that was not measured on this panel.

\subsection{No-trade band}
\impl{\code{EquityKellyMIO.domain_constraints: band_*}}
The band size and sell floor are
\begin{equation*}
n_i=\ell\Big\lceil\frac{\beta\cdot10^{-4}\max(p_ih_i,\,m)}{p_i\,\ell}\Big\rceil\ \ (0\text{ for a mandatory exit}),\qquad
f_i=\min(n_i,h_i).
\end{equation*}
With the trade room $T_i=\max(\lfloor\bar x_i/p_i\rfloor+h_i,\,n_i,\,1)$, the
band constraints are
\begin{align}
n_i a_i-n_i(1-d_i) &\le b_i && \text{(buy floor)}\\
f_i a_i-f_i d_i &\le s_i && \text{(sell floor)}\\
b_i+s_i &\le T_i a_i && \text{(no trade unless active)}
\end{align}

\subsection{Objective}
\begin{equation}
\max\ \sum_{o\in\Om}\omega_o\,t_o .
\end{equation}

\section{Post-solve recomputation and refusals}
\impl{\code{ScenarioUtilitySolve.extract}; \code{EquityKellyMIO.validate_inputs}}

\paragraph{Before the solve.} \code{EquityKellyMIO.validate_inputs}
refuses the solve if any of the following holds:
\begin{itemize}
\item the bundle digest, producer or model manifest does not match its pin;
\item the portfolio is malformed, for example fractional or negative
  holdings;
\item an uncertainty envelope is not admitted for $t$ and the release (stale,
  from after the decision, the wrong unit or model, or coverage below
  \code{uncertainty_min_coverage});
\item the cap artifact fails \code{ConfirmedCaps.problems} or its pins;
\item \code{deployment_mode} and \code{deployment_eligible} are
  inconsistent;
\item the cap is stale;
\item the cap's release differs from the bundle's.
\end{itemize}
The two cap-timing refusals (the cap is from the future, and the cap was
generated after the decision) are skipped only under
\code{cap_evidence_look_ahead} $=$ true, which is allowed only when
\code{deployment_mode} is false. Before building the program,
\code{build_model} also refuses:
\begin{itemize}
\item $W^0\le 0$;
\item $\underline W\le 0$, or $\underline W\ge\overline W$;
\item weights that do not sum to 1;
\item more than $S_{\max}$ scenarios;
\item a non-finite $r_{io}$.
\end{itemize}
If no name is eligible and nothing is held, the solver is not called and the
target is zero.

\paragraph{After the solve.} The solve runs on HiGHS (\code{appsi_highs})
with \code{mip_rel_gap}$=0$, \code{threads}$=1$ and
\code{random_seed}$=0$. Any termination other than \code{optimal} refuses,
and so does an integer variable that comes back fractional. From the integer
solution, \code{extract} recomputes $c^+$, the buying power, the gross
exposure, the cardinality, the minimum ticket (buys only) and every $W_o$,
and refuses any violation. It also refuses any $W_o\le 0$. CVaR is recomputed
exactly as a weighted $\alpha$-quantile $\eta^\star$, with
$\text{CVaR}=\eta^\star+\sum_o\omega_o(\ell_o-\eta^\star)^+/(1-\alpha)$
(\code{_weighted_cvar}), and checked against $C$ when a cap is set.

\paragraph{Outputs.} The solve returns:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{target} $\{i:Q_i\}$;
\item \code{trades} $\{i:(b_i,s_i)\}$;
\item \code{cash_after} $=c^+$;
\item \code{metrics}: objective, exact expected utility, holding and trade
  counts, gross exposure, CVaR and its $\eta$, and the minimum and maximum
  $W_o$;
\item \code{evidence}: routing reasons, the uncertainty admission, the
  look-ahead disclosure, and the \code{SolveRecord} (status, gap, seconds,
  binding rows).
\end{itemize}
How a refusal surfaces depends on its exception. A pre-solve refusal
(\code{ValueError}) or a non-optimal termination (\code{RuntimeError}) becomes
a \code{mio_refused} row, and that lead group trades nothing at that tick.
A post-solve recompute violation (\code{AssertionError}: a fractional integer,
cash, buying power, gross exposure, cardinality, minimum ticket, CVaR, or a
non-positive $W_o$) is \emph{not} caught by \code{MioDecider.decide}, so it
aborts the whole simulation run.

\section{Backtest integration}\label{sec:integration}
\impl{\code{MioDecider.decide}; \code{EquityReplay.read_entry / evaluate}}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Per tick.} At each 30-minute lattice instant $t$ carrying a
  bundle, \code{EquityReplay.read_entry} first books any cash flow due
  by $t$. A contribution is available to a decision at the same instant.
  \code{evaluate} then applies all forced exits, then all entries due
  at this bar, and finally calls \code{MioDecider.decide}$(t,\text{portfolio})$.
\item \textbf{Per lead group.} Lead groups are solved one at a time in
  \emph{ascending} $L$ (\code{MioDeciderNode}). Each group gets its own
  \code{EquityKellyMIO}, pinned to that group's bundle digest and the
  release's cap and manifest.
\item \textbf{Shared cash.} The first group sees $c_0=B_0=$ live replay
  cash. Each later group sees the previous group's \code{cash_after}, the
  planned cash after that group's buys. This order is a disclosed allocation
  bias toward short leads. A refused group leaves the cash unchanged.
\item \textbf{Open lots.} Every bundle row whose name holds an open lot is
  dropped (skip reason \code{open_lot_at_decision}). The MIO therefore
  always sees \code{positions}$=\{\}$, so $h_i=0$, $s_i=0$, $f_i=0$, and
  the inventory and sell-side path is not exercised. Orders are buys only;
  a sell raises.
\item \textbf{Fills.} Fills happen at the next bar's \code{open}
  (\code{fill_bar_offset}$=1$), as market orders with no partial fills.
  The fee is $\kappa^b$ or $\kappa^s$ at the fill price times the quantity.
  A buy whose price times quantity plus fee exceeds the replay's cash is
  refused as \code{insufficient_cash}. That can happen because the MIO
  priced at the decision close.
\item \textbf{Exits.} A lot is force-exited at the \code{open} of the bar
  $L$ bars after its fill bar, at the sell cost. This is not decided by the
  optimizer.
\item \textbf{Releases.} Each fold $k$ is a new \code{EquityReplay}
  segment, and its cash carries forward through
  \code{CashFlowPolicy.segment}. The biweekly phase is global across
  segments. A segment that ends with an open lot refuses.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Known unimplemented or unrealistic assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Not modelled: T+1 settlement, good-faith violations, freeriding, PDT
  and wash sales. Sale proceeds are reusable at once.
\item Halts are not modelled (\code{halted}$=$false). An absent minute is an
  absent bar.
\item Prices are split-adjusted, so integer-share sizing is distorted before
  later splits.
\item The costs are illustrative Schwab figures, not broker pins. The
  optimizer prices at the decision close, and fills happen at the next
  open.
\item There is no slippage beyond the half-spread, no market impact, no
  latency and no partial fills.
\item The gate admission (tradable set, leads, caps) uses all 20 folds, so
  the results are developmental post-selection and
  \code{deployment_eligible}$=$false.
\item The warmup hyperparameter search reads only rows before 2022-05-01, but
  its cohort, each lead's scored names and the horizon weights come from the
  Gate-3 admission, which was decided on the same 2022--2025 folds the
  simulation trades. The traded window therefore chose which names and leads
  were tuned (disclosed look-ahead).
\item $\tilde\pi_i$ is not a bound, and its attested attainment floor is
  synthetic.
\item The sequential lead-group allocation is myopic. There is no joint
  multi-lead solve.
\item Forecasts are the release models' batch predictions over their
  validation window. They are not per-tick calls to a persisted model.
\item The inventory, sell-side and no-trade-band logic is not exercised,
  because every decision starts from an empty book.
\end{itemize}
