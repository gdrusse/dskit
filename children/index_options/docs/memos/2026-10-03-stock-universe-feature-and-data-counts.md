# Stock universe: features and data counts per ticker and expiry (2026-10-03)

## TL;DR

The 116-stock universe now has two onboarded feature tables (daily volume and
market-relative features, 2016 to 2026-09-30; option-trade features,
2024-01-19 to 2026-08-26) and a raw option-trade bar stream of 4,439,385 bars
covering expiries 31 to 45 calendar days out. The per-ticker counts below are
read straight from those stores. The steps 1 to 3 availability run (target
dates, panel, admission, folds) for all 116 tickers has NOT run yet, so this
memo has no fold counts or admitted-feature counts.

## What was counted

- Command: `memo_counts.py` (session scratchpad, read-only), reading the two
  feature tables as handed off and the bar stream with
  `dskit.onboarding.observations.iter_stream` (winners only; 1:32 wall time,
  2.1 GB peak RSS).
- Daily features: source `stock-daily-features-r2`, built from
  `stock-daily-bars` (manifest `be101b08…`, Yahoo split-adjusted OHLCV) and
  `benchmark-daily-bars` (SPY, `c6aaf795…`).
- Option-trade features: source `stock-option-trade-features-r2`
  (strike basis = as-traded close from `AsTradedClose`, ADR-0234;
  strict-prior label, so a row dated t holds the trades of the previous
  session).
- Bars: store `option-universe-100` (`options/bars`), traded contracts only,
  DTE 31 to 45, no quotes, IV, bid or ask. 117 tickers: HES is in the bars
  but excluded from the universe (delisted mid-window).

Terms: *DTE* is calendar days from trade date to expiry. *Monthly* is the
share of bars on third-Friday expiries. *Null share* is the share of rows
where the feature is undefined (warm-up windows for daily features; thin
trading for the straddle ratio).

## Not run, and known limits

- **Steps 1 to 3 for 116 tickers: not run.** Blocked in session by a tool
  permission check. Planned as a generic `--through step3` flag on
  `workflow` / `workflow-batch` plus a cross-ticker availability rollup; not
  built. Until then there are no target-date, fold or admitted-feature
  counts per ticker.
- `vl_log_volume` is on the split-adjusted share basis, so its level for
  pre-split dates moves when a later split lands (hindsight). Dollar volume,
  the volume ratio and Amihud are unaffected.
- Moneyness across a spin-off booked as a fractional split (GE, HON, FDX,
  CMCSA, DHR) is approximate (ADR-0234).
- The straddle ratio is null on 7.1% of rows: no in-band strike with both a
  call and a put traded at one expiry.
- Bars dated on a Saturday (2024-06-01, 1,227 bars) have no next session and
  are dropped from the strict-prior table.
