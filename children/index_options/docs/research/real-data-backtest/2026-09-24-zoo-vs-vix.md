# First real-data test: can the z21 distribution rungs beat the option market?

## Question

On real SPX closes (Cboe, 1975-2026) with VIX as the market's implied scale,
do the ADR-0181 scale rungs forecast the 21-trading-day terminal distribution
better than a naive rung, better than VIX alone, and does choosing condor
strikes from them earn more than choosing strikes from VIX?

## Setup

- Zoo `configs/run-real-zoo.json` (ADR-0097 staged, ADR-0182), six rungs,
  32 expanding walk-forward folds, val years 1995-2026, 35-day embargo,
  twCRPS on |z| in [0.5, 2.5]; paired fold differences, HAC lag 1,
  Bonferroni over 15 pairs.
- Backtest `CondorBacktest`: one condor every 21 trading days per fold
  (non-overlapping), shorts at the forecast 10%/90% quantiles, wings 0.5 z
  beyond, SPX 5-pt strikes rounded outward, $0.65/leg, PM settlement at
  close x exp(label). Legs priced by `VolIndexSmileQuotes` calibrated to the
  2026-09-23 SPXW chain (+-0.005 IV); half-spread max(0.20, 0.5%).
  Three books on identical prices: `model` (enter iff forecast EV > 0),
  `always` (model strikes every entry), `implied` (VIX-lognormal strikes).

## Findings

| rung | twCRPS | model book n / mean $ | always - implied $ (t) |
|---|---|---|---|
| empirical (R0a) | 0.3297 | 115 / -149 | -67 (-1.15) |
| log-HAR | 0.3228 | 149 / +38 | -31 (-0.51) |
| LightGBM | 0.3226 | 138 / +7 | -52 (-0.82) |
| VIX only | 0.3221 | 324 / +7 | -57 (-0.79) |
| HAR + VIX | 0.3219 | 311 / +19 | -57 (-0.81) |
| LightGBM + VIX | 0.3231 | 277 / +1 | -80 (-1.10) |

- Every conditional rung beats the empirical rung (p <= 0.0005, all five
  pairs reject). No conditional pair differs detectably; the frontier picks
  HAR + VIX as simplest-not-detectably-worse, but VIX alone is within 0.0002.
  **Realized-vol history adds nothing detectable over VIX.**
- Implied (VIX) strikes: 369 trades, +$79/trade, t = 1.32, hit 92%, CVaR5
  -$3,795. Model-strike books are ~$0-40/trade with worse tails (CVaR5
  ~-$7,000). **No rung's strikes beat the market's strikes.**
- A flat-VIX proxy (first pass) showed +$400-500/trade for every book; that
  was a pricing artifact (OTM calls ~2x overpriced, deep puts ~3x
  underpriced vs the real chain). The smile calibration removed it.

## Limits

One-day smile calibration applied to 31 years (skew varies by regime);
proxy spreads; SPXW expiries did not exist every 21 trading days before
2016; overlapping-horizon diagnostics (PIT/Berkowitz) are not tests.

## Next
