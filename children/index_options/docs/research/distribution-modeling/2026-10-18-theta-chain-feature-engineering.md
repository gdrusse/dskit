## Question

How should the ThetaData end-of-day option chains (frozen export, 427 committed Parquet files, 118-name eligible cohort, development 2016-2019, validation 2020-2023) be engineered into features that a small distributional model (mixture-density MLP / GRU / PatchTST head under `TorchCDF`, loss CRPS or a composite with a threshold term) can use to improve the forecast of the 31-day standardized log return `z = log(P_T/P_0)/reference_scale`, `reference_scale = max(rv_22, 0.001) * sqrt(sessions_to_expiry)` (ADR-0269/0270), and in particular the mass around the strike grid `Z = {-3, -2.5, ..., 3}`?

Scope: read-only research. No fit, no market record dated >= 2024-01-01 decoded; the only data touched were projected columns of the frozen export `/home/russell/data/index_options/theta-strike-screen-20261018/export-frozen/theta` on quote dates 2017-03-01..2017-05-31 and 2018-10-01..2018-12-31 for eight names (AAPL, AEO, AG, ALB, AMKR, ABUS; probe script kept outside the repository). Every number below from that probe is labelled "probe".

## Finding

### 0. Facts the design has to respect (codebase and data)

- **Target and scale.** `z` is the standardized terminal return; `reference_scale` is written at `children/index_options/index_options/cdf_study.py:3528` from `rv_22` (annualized-free, per-session realized vol) times `sqrt(sessions_to_expiry)`. A strike `K` maps to `z_K = log(K/spot)/reference_scale` (`cdf_study.py:793-796`, `DecisionRegionContextBuilder`; ADR-0270 section 1, `docs/architecture/decision-log.md:31408`). Any option-implied quantity that is to live on the same axis as the model output must therefore be converted to log-moneyness and divided by the SAME `reference_scale` row value; this is exactly how the older raw-chain proxy quantiles were standardized (`cdf_study.py:3531-3534`: `rows[rn_q_*] / rows.reference_scale`).
- **Existing owners.** `dskit/pipeline/libs/chain_features.py` (`ChainDayFeatures`, class at line 339) already solves the parity forward from mids (`QuoteBook.solve_forward`, lines 202-215, via `OptionPriceCDF.parity_forwards`), Black-76 IV per quote (`QuoteBook.iv`, 217-227), the 25-delta wings by linear-in-delta interpolation (`wing`, 235-250), `put_call_iv_spread` (`spread`, 252-262), the ATM pick, straddle ratio and half-spread (`_smile`, 745-763), OI/volume totals and lagged OI change (`_positioning`, 765-788), `term_slope` (`_term`, 857-866), and the stale-vendor-spot flag (`_stale`, 844-855). It selects ONE listed expiry per target DTE, the nearest within a tolerance (`_pick`, 701-710); it never interpolates across expiries. The nine admitted fields and their units are `children/index_options/configs/theta-ml-feature-families.json` (families `theta_volatility`, `theta_skew`, `theta_liquidity`; OI withheld; `max_age_days` 7, `strict_prior` true). The older exact-expiry route, `RawChainFeatureBuilder` (`cdf_study.py:2237-2355`), emits order-invariant contract tensors (`NODE_FIELDS` line 2240: log_moneyness, iv, log_rel_spread, log_oi, log_depth) and risk-neutral proxy quantiles `rn_q_pppp` (`_qname` 2263, `_proxy` 2281 via `OptionPriceCDF`, `dskit/pipeline/libs/predictive_cdf.py:7588`) plus `rn_median`, `rn_iqr`, `rn_left_width`, `rn_right_width` (`cdf_study.py:3536-3540`). `OptionImpliedTransportCDF` (`predictive_cdf.py:1814`) already transports those quantiles to a physical CDF through a training-only PIT map. Event detection from the front-IV crush exists (`dskit/pipeline/libs/chain_events.py:1-45`).
- **Loss side.** `TorchCDF` composes `nll`, `crps`, `tail_crps`, `decision_brier`, `decision_log`, `wing_twcrps` (`predictive_cdf.py:4217-4219`); `decision_brier` evaluates the mixture CDF at the declared thresholds and squares the error against `1{y <= k}` (`predictive_cdf.py:4114-4127`). A feature that IS the market's `F_Q(z_k)` at those same thresholds gives the head a one-weight path to the Brier term; nothing else in the 42 price features does.
- **R5 evidence on the existing nine** (`children/index_options/docs/memos/2026-10-15-theta-amendment6.md:330-348`): on the 2019 warm-up fold the volatility family alone improved row-weighted weighted-CRPS by +0.56 % over price-only, liquidity -0.15 %, skew -0.14 %, all three -0.33 %; on 2020 the one completed option arm (liquidity) improved +0.09 %. Every 2019 neural arm lost to the per-symbol empirical reference. Theta coverage on that panel was sparse (85/319 symbols with any `theta_atm_iv_30`; ADR-0270 section 2). The signal, if any, is small and lives in the volatility level; this is consistent with the literature below and with the fact that the nine features were date-keyed 30/90-DTE descriptors with a strictly prior quote date, not the model's horizon.
- **Chain geometry (probe, 2017-2018 dates).** Rows per symbol-day: AAPL 1,620 (max 1,900), AEO 644, AG 444, ALB 298, AMKR 146, ABUS 27. Listed expiries per day: 16 / 12 / 11 / 6 / 4 / 4. An expiry exactly 31 calendar days out exists on 20 % of days for weekly names (AAPL, AEO, AG) and 5 % for monthly-only names (ALB, AMKR, ABUS); the bracketing expiries are typically 29/35 DTE (weeklies) or 17/45 DTE (monthlies). Strikes at the expiry nearest 31 DTE: 50 / 33 / 21 / 21 / 17 / 3. Zero-bid share at that expiry: 13 % / 31 % / 39 % / 30 % / 42 % / 45 %; no null or crossed quotes in the probe. Vendor `implied_vol` missing or non-positive on 9-14 % of rows (the ADR-0265 reason the owner re-solves IV from mids). Rows with positive `volume`/`count`: 51 % / 7 % / 10 % / 24 % / 7 % / 5 %; positive `open_interest`: 73 % / 26 % / 29 % / 61 % / 31 % / 51 %. **Strike spacing in 31-day-sigma units** (ATM step / spot / (vendor ATM IV x sqrt(DTE/365))): AAPL 0.16, AEO 0.28, AG 0.51, ALB 0.54, AMKR 0.99, ABUS 2.05. The model's threshold step is 0.5 sigma: half the probed names cannot resolve the grid from their own strikes.
- **Clocks (probe).** The option `timestamp` column is midnight (date only) on ~85 % of rows and 15:58-15:59 ET on the rest; `underlying_timestamp` clusters at 16:00-17:45 ET (AAPL modes 17:29, 17:19, 17:38; AEO 16:41, 17:45; ABUS 16:00). ThetaData builds its national EOD report at 17:15 ET from the last NBBO OPRA reported by then, and states that OPRA publishes open interest once a day at about 06:30 ET for the END OF THE PREVIOUS trading day ([option_history_eod](https://docs.thetadata.us/operations_python/option_history_eod.html), [option_history_open_interest](https://docs.thetadata.us/operations_excel/option_history_open_interest.html)). So: the quotes on row date `D` are a 16:00-ish close NBBO (stale on illiquid contracts), `underlying_price` on row `D` is a POST-close print that can include after-hours trading (an after-the-close earnings reaction would be in it while the quotes are pre-announcement), and `open_interest` on row `D` describes the close of `D-1` and was publicly known from 06:30 ET on `D`. ADR-0270 section 2 (`decision-log.md:31417`) independently records that `underlying_price` is the UNADJUSTED basis and the chain-features owner documents days on which it is a value copied from an earlier date (`chain_features.py:44-52`).

### 1. Ranked, bounded feature inventory (26 inputs in 29 numbered rows, six families)

Conventions used in every row: **inputs** are projected columns of the export plus the panel's `spot` (split-adjusted close), `rv_22`, `reference_scale`, `sessions_to_expiry`; **horizon** `h` is the row's own calendar DTE (31) and `T = h/365`; **r** is the ALFRED DGS3MO vintage already onboarded (ADR-0270 section 2); **IV** means the owner's own Black-76 IV on the parity forward, never the vendor column; **lag** is the row availability relative to the origin close; **owner** names the code that computes it today or "new". "Standardize by scale" means divide a log-moneyness or a vol by the row's `reference_scale` (or by `rv_22 * sqrt(252)` for an annualized vol) so the number is on the model's z axis and comparable across names and regimes; "own history" means a fit-window-only robust z against the symbol's trailing distribution, which the inventory uses only where the level has no natural z-axis meaning (flow and liquidity).

### 2. Which representation helps a small model most

Three candidates: (a) 10-12 scalar summaries; (b) a fixed 13-vector `F_Q(z_k)` on the threshold grid (plus a usable-strike count); (c) raw surface tensors (`RawChainFeatureBuilder` nodes, 5 channels x N contracts, set-encoded).

- **Sample size.** The development panel is about 59k rows (118 names x ~125 origins/yr x 4 yrs; the 2016-2020 118-cohort panel in the memo is 59,260 base rows, `2026-10-15-theta-amendment6.md:259`). Labels overlap 31 calendar days (about 8 non-overlapping windows per name-year) and the cross-section shares market months, so the independent information is closer to a few hundred name-months than 59k. ADR-0270's capacity rule (parameters <= 0.25 x fit rows, encoders at 8-16k parameters) already binds. A set encoder over contracts (c) has to learn moneyness geometry, bid-ask noise and the Breeden-Litzenberger derivative from data that the pricing identity gives for free; it is the representation most likely to fit the 2016-2019 regime and the one the capacity rule leaves least room for. The 2026-09-28 exact-expiry work on SPY/QQQ/IWM used (c) with a different sample; the R5 result that NO option arm beat the empirical reference in 2019 argues for fewer, better-conditioned inputs, not more.
- **The loss.** CRPS integrates `(F - 1{y <= z})^2` over `z`; `decision_brier` samples it at `Z`. A forecaster that receives `F_Q(z_k)` can reach "market CDF plus a learned monotone correction" with a near-identity map, and the PIT-recalibration literature says that correction is low-dimensional and stable (a beta/two-parameter map in [Fackler & King 1990](https://doi.org/10.2307/1242339) and [Bliss & Panigirtzoglou 2004](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00637.x); 21 transport knots in the codebase's `market_calibrated` reference, ADR-0270 section 5). That is the strongest argument for (b): it turns the problem from "learn a conditional CDF" into "learn a small deviation from a strong, outcome-free prior". Scalars (a) cannot express the asymmetric tail mass at 1.5-3 sigma that the Brier term scores; they can express width and skew, which is why they rank first for GLOBAL CRPS.
- **Physical vs risk-neutral.** The gap `F_P - F_Q` is the pricing kernel's work: a variance premium (Q wider than P, [Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou 2009](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhp008)), a left-tail premium (Q left tail heavier, [Bollerslev, Todorov & Xu 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2015.02.010)) and a U-shaped kernel on individual names ([Christoffersen, Heston & Jacobs 2013](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hht033)). All three are smooth, slowly varying functions of `k` and of the state (VRP, skew), which is a textbook case for a small MLP with `F_Q(z_k)` AND the family-A scalars as inputs: the scalars tell it how much to shrink, the vector tells it where. Feeding the vector alone forces the net to infer the state from 13 correlated numbers.
- **Resolution honesty.** On names whose strike step exceeds 0.5 sigma the 13-vector is an interpolation of 4-6 real points; the usable-strike count (28) must accompany it, and set B should be scored separately on the resolvable subset (step <= 0.5 sigma, declared before any outcome is read) so a null result on thin names does not hide skill on liquid ones. In the probe this split is roughly AAPL/AEO vs AG/ALB/AMKR/ABUS.

Recommendation: **(a) + (b)**, never (c) at this sample size. Represent (b) as `logit(F_Q(z_k)) - logit(Phi(k))` (deviation from the standard normal the scale already implies, so an all-zero vector means "market agrees with the price-only scale"), with missing values left `None` for the existing fit-only median imputation and a per-row missing companion, exactly the `ParquetPanelAugment` contract (`dskit/pipeline/libs/parquet_panel.py:34, 53, 75-78`).

### 3. Leakage and clock rules

1. **Quote clock.** Export quotes are the last NBBO at the 17:15 ET report, in practice the 16:00 close NBBO (probe: timestamps 15:58-15:59 where present). Options cease trading at 16:00 ET with the stock, so a feature computed from row `D` is available at the close of `D` and is admissible for an origin whose `spot` is the close of `D`, PROVIDED the decision is modelled as "after the close, indicative" (the ADR-0270 market stage already declares `clock after_date_close_indicative_not_executable`). Any claim of an executable same-day fill is excluded (ADR-0269 section 4: "own-day EOD chain features cannot support an earlier same-day fill").
2. **Same-day vs strictly prior.** The current admission uses `strict_prior` true with `max_age_days` 7 (`theta-ml-feature-families.json`; `run-theta-ml-panel.json:165-166, 225-226`; semantics in `dskit/pipeline/libs/observation_tables.py:11-14`): the option row attached to origin `D` is dated `D-1` or earlier. That is one session more conservative than the quote clock requires and costs the features their most recent session (IV moves most on event days). Proposal for the screen: keep `strict_prior` true as the DEFAULT arm (nothing changes in the audited bridge) and register ONE same-day arm (`strict_prior` false, `max_age_days` 0) as an explicitly separate recipe, scored on the same identities; the difference is the price of the conservative clock. `ParquetPanelAugment` refuses `strict_prior` != true today (`parquet_panel.py:75-76`), so the same-day arm needs its own ADR-approved parameter, not a config edit.
3. **`underlying_price` is not a close.** Its timestamps are 16:00-17:45 ET (probe) and it is unadjusted and sometimes stale (`chain_features.py:44-52`; ADR-0270 section 2). It must never enter a feature as the spot of origin `D`: for an after-the-close announcer it contains the reaction. Use it ONLY to put strikes and the panel spot on one share basis (next item); use the parity forward for moneyness, as the owner does.
4. **Corporate-action basis.** Strikes and `underlying_price` are unadjusted; the panel `spot`, `terminal_return` and `rv_22` are split-adjusted bars (ADR-0269 "Important inventory fact"; `PriceCalendarCDFPanel`, `cdf_study.py:3754`). The z-grid evaluation `K_k = spot * exp(k * reference_scale)` must use the UNADJUSTED spot of day `D` on the chain side, obtained from the parity forward discounted at `r` (not from `underlying_price`), and then `F_Q(K_k)` is basis-free because `k` is. Rows inside a split/adjustment window keep the panel's existing corporate-action exclusions (`CorporateActionRule`, `cdf_study.py:2570`). Dividends: the forward `F = K + e^{rT}(C - P)` already nets the expected dividend; a feature built from `F/spot` carries `-qT`, so compare log-moneyness to the FORWARD, never to spot, when a dividend falls inside `h`. The target is a price return, not total return, so the implied drift (`rn_median`) legitimately differs from zero by the dividend yield; do not "correct" it.
5. **Open interest.** Row `D` OI = close of `D-1`, published 06:30 ET on `D` (vendor docs). It is admissible on `D` under both clocks, but the contract is unverified in the export; verification = compare a sample of rows against an independent OI print before admission (the ADR-0269 withholding). Its change feature lags two sessions.
6. **Rates.** The ALFRED DGS3MO vintage is a same-day join with counted exclusions (ADR-0270 gap 4); a strictly prior rate is the conservative choice and costs nothing at a 31-day horizon (a 5 bp rate change moves the forward by 0.0004 %).
7. **Development-window-only statistics.** Every "own-history z" (families D, E), every imputation median, the PIT/transport map, and the resolvable-subset threshold (strike step in sigma) are computed on fit rows only, per fold, exactly as the existing fit-only median imputation and the `market_calibrated` transport (fitted on rows settling before 2020-01-01). Ratios to `rv_22` and to `reference_scale` need no history and are preferred for that reason. The z grid is fixed in advance (ADR-0270 section 1); the usable-strike gate values are declared in the registration before any outcome is read.
8. **Label purge.** Unchanged: 40-origin monitor band, settlement-reaching purge, holdout lock 2021-01-01 for this screen, 2024+ never decoded, 2026 sealed (ADR-0270 section 2). An option feature dated `D` never reads a quote dated after `D` (the two-expiry interpolation reads the same day's two expiries, never a later day's).

### 4. First screen: set A, set B, and what each needs

**Set A (existing 9, zero new computation).** `theta_atm_iv_30/90`, `risk_reversal_30`, `put_call_iv_spread_30`, `term_slope_30`, `half_spread_over_premium_30`, `straddle_ratio_30`, `volume_call/put_30`, strictly prior, 7-day age. Already in `run-theta-mixture-grid.json` arm `price_plus_volatility_skew_liquidity` (51 inputs). Known weakness: 30-DTE "nearest within tolerance", not the row's horizon; absolute vols, not ratios to `rv_22`. Cheapest improvement inside set A without a new node: add the two ratios `theta_atm_iv_30 / (rv_22 sqrt(252))` and `risk_reversal_30 / theta_atm_iv_30` through the existing per-row arithmetic kind (`ComputeFields`, `dskit/pipeline/kinds_compute.py`) in the panel document; call it A+ (11 inputs).

**Set B (A+ plus families A-h, B, C, E-gates, F; 26 option inputs).** New computation, in dependency order:

1. `ChainDayFeatures` horizon target: a `bracket` mode in `_pick` (two expiries around `dte`), per-expiry ATM IV, wings, forward and usable-strike counts for both, plus the variance-time interpolation to `h` (features 1, 3, 4, 5, 28, 29). Pure arithmetic over the existing `QuoteBook`; the memory and streaming contract is unchanged.
2. A tier-2 `ImpliedMoments` step over the same `QuoteBook` (features 7, 8, 9): three weighted sums per expiry with the `min_wing_nodes` / gap gates reused from `OptionPriceCDF`.
3. A tier-2 "implied CDF at declared z" evaluation (features 10-23): `OptionPriceCDF.estimate` per bracketing expiry, strikes `K_k` from the row's `reference_scale` and the parity forward, per-strike IV interpolation across the two expiries in total variance, re-pricing to a CDF. The grid is a param (never a literal); the output is 13 probabilities + a usable-strike count + the two existing spread summaries. ADR-0270 gap 1 (per-threshold scoring node, `StrikeGridScores`) is the natural sibling: the same `z`-grid vocabulary on the scoring side.
4. Panel augmentation: the new columns join through the existing `ParquetPanelAugment` bridge with age/missing companions, strictly prior by default; the `reference_scale` dependency means step 3 runs AFTER the frozen price panel exists (it needs the row's scale), i.e. as a second augmentation table keyed by (symbol, quote_date), not inside the chain panel.

**Cost per 118-name year of chains.** Evidence: `ChainPanelBuild` consumed 2,144,702 rows for 86 roots over 2019-12-16..2020-01-31 (about 32 sessions, 2,723 symbol-dates) and built the panel in 22.48 s (`/home/russell/data/index_options/theta-ml-prep-20261016/chain-run-r3.log`, "node panel: ok in 22.483s"; memo line 227), i.e. about 790 rows per symbol-day and about 95k rows/s including the per-quote Newton IV solves; the full 2016-2020 118-cohort chain preparation took 622 s for 93 raw-present roots (memo line 259), about 125 s per cohort-year. Probe medians (AAPL 1,620, mid names 300-650, thin 30-150 rows/day) agree with the 790 average. So one 118-name year is roughly 118 x 252 x 800 = 24M rows; at the measured 95k rows/s about 250 s for the existing node; set B adds, per symbol-day, one more expiry's IV solves (about x1.5 solves) plus O(strikes) sums (negligible) plus two `OptionPriceCDF.estimate` calls (isotonic fit on <= 100 points, milliseconds): estimate 2-3x the baseline, about 10-15 minutes per 118-name year, about one hour for 2016-2019 plus 2020, single process, CPU, well inside the ADR-0270 4-hour envelope. Memory: one root at a time (`chain_panel.py:1-20`), about 61 B per held row, so AAPL at 1,900 rows/day x 1,260 days is about 150 MB. Disk: 26 option columns x 59k rows is < 20 MB per panel.

**Screen design (inherits ADR-0270's funnel; cells counted, not launched here).** Arms: price_only (control), A (existing 9), A+ (11), B-minus (families A-h, B, E, F: 13 scalars), B (26). Encoders mlp and patchtst_hf32 only (the two the ADR-0270 D-L1T design already pairs with the theta arm); losses L1 (crps + tail_crps) and L3 (crps + decision_brier at the `Z` edges), since C's whole point is the threshold term. That is 5 arms x 2 encoders x 2 losses = 20 cells per fold, 40 neural fits on folds 2/3, below ADR-0270's Stage-2 scale once its D-L2/D-L4 recipes are dropped. Reports: global CRPS skill vs horizon_empirical, Brier at each `k`, both on the full cohort and on the pre-declared resolvable subset; paired with the `market_raw` / `market_calibrated` references on the M24 identities so that "model with C" is compared with "market plus 21-knot recalibration", which is the honest baseline for family C.

### 5. Non-goals and risks

- **Not a trading claim.** Better CRPS or Brier at `Z` is a forecast property; exercise, settlement, costs and fills remain in the decision layer (ADR-0269 section 1).
- **Overfitting to a handful of correlated features.** Families A and B are near-collinear (ATM IV, MF variance, straddle ratio all measure width); the sweep should admit ONE width measure per arm and treat the rest as replacements, not additions; with the capacity cap binding, every added input shifts the 8-16k parameters, and 40 fits over 2 folds inspected by one reader is already a multiple-testing problem ([Harvey, Liu & Zhu 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhv059)); keep the date-block bootstrap and the fixed promotion rule.
- **Double counting with price features.** `rv_1/5/22/66`, `down/up_rv`, `momentum` and `vl_*` are already inputs; `log_vrp_h` and `iv_h_over_rv` are deliberately RATIOS to `rv_22` so the net sees the increment, not a second copy of realized vol; `straddle_ratio_30` (existing) is `iv x sqrt(T)` in disguise and should be dropped when `iv_h_over_rv` is admitted.
- **Regime dependence.** 2020 (the scored fold) contains the March VRP spike and the fastest IV mean reversion in the sample; a feature whose sign is learned on 2016-2019 (low-vol, steady VRP) will be tested on an out-of-support range of `iv_h_over_rv`. Report skill with and without 2020-02-15..2020-05-31 origins as a descriptive split declared in advance, never as a selection.
- **Thin chains.** Half the probed names cannot resolve the 0.5-sigma grid; family C on them is the tail-completion model. The resolvable-subset report and the usable-strike gate are mandatory, and a null on the whole cohort does not refute C on liquid names (nor the reverse).
- **American exercise and borrow.** Single-stock puts carry early-exercise value and hard-to-borrow names carry a negative implied dividend; `F_Q` from mids is a European/risk-neutral object ([Barone-Adesi & Whaley 1987](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1987.tb02569.x)); the model is expected to absorb the bias through the learned transport, and `pc_iv_spread` will partly measure borrow rather than information.
- **Vendor spot and OI.** `underlying_price` is a post-close, unadjusted, sometimes stale print: never a spot; OI stays withheld until its publication contract is verified in the export, not in the docs.
- **Clock arms.** The same-day arm (section 3.2) is a protocol change that needs its own approval; the screen is valid with the strictly prior default alone.
- **No code was written for this research**; each new node (horizon bracket, implied moments, implied CDF at z, StrikeGridScores) needs its own sweep, ADR approval, TDD and review per the working agreement.
