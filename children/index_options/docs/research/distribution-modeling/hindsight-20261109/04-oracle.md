# Bounded hindsight oracle and universe base rates (development quarters, in-sample)

Universe per decision week: every eligible long single contract (documents' quote rule) in the quoted (symbol, expiry) groups with 1-60 DTE, bought at the next session's ask, held to expiry, settled at the expiry close over the basis factor (the ledger's rule). `ret` is P&L per premium. Short structures are not enumerated (see the note).
