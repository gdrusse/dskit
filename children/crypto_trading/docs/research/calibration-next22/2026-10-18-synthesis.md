## Question
Can new training objectives, architectures and local calibration improve prediction of 15-minute crypto settlement beyond market probabilities and the earlier sparse residual model?

## Finding
Completed 20 declared approaches plus two penalty-scale controls: 22 new JSON pipelines, 132 outputs and 2200 asset/lead/bin cells. Ten improve over raw market overall, six over simple calibration, none over prior ARD under cumulative37-method exploratory screens. Sparse Newton log-loss method24 has the best mean score (-0.000981 raw; -0.000177 priorARD), but the latter adjustedp=.2069. Four of14 raw-market method/cell hits survive seven-day sensitivity; no cell clears recalibrated-market or priorARD comparisons. ETH high-probability bins at5m/8m retain the clearest market-only miscalibration. Preserve the frozen future protocol and one challenger for a separately declared fresh test; historical reuse is not confirmation.
