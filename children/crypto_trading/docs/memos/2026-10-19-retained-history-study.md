## Interpretation and limits

For a selected purchase, modeled net per share is settled payout minus assumed entry ask minus assumed fee. Kalshi uses the historical candle quote, which does not prove a delayed fill or standing depth. Native BTC uses later displayed books with source and purchased-side freshness gates, but does not prove matching, queue position, cancellations, or historical fee vintage. No result is realized trading profit.

The declared family has **43,200,000** comparisons: 600 side/cells × 100 method/control/diagnostic labels × 18 scenarios × eight reserved windows × five endpoints. With family error budget 0.05, a one-sided raw p-value must be below `0.05 / 43,200,000 = 1.16 × 10⁻⁹` for an adjusted p below 0.05. Both day and fixed seven-day cluster checks, adequate samples, and enough wins and losses are also required. Bonferroni allows dependent hypotheses; the cluster estimates remain approximations. Three old native alternate-scenario t-flags are all-win, zero-loss cases and are not promoted.

W4/W5 were previously examined. October 8–9 had computation-exposed labels/features, and the remaining October gap lacks a complete no-read attestation. The added native book hour also has previously parsed terminal labels. Thus a positive historical mean is a **research lead**, not new holdout confirmation. The next decisive data are a separately admitted, genuinely uninspected interval with native outcomes and time-stamped decision/entry quotes and fees; the two protected protocols cannot be opened early.
