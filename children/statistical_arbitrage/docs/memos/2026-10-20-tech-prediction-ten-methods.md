## TL;DR

Ten pre-registered additions to the ADR-0272 lagged-spread model, now drawing on Theta's EOD Greeks, vendor IV, open interest, volume, quote sizes, term structure and stock realized volatility, were tuned on 2020 and validated once on 2021–2025. **On the primary next-session label none meets the pre-registered "improves the model" rule** (pooled R²oos above the baseline, BH-rejected Diebold–Mariano, and a per-fold win in at least four of five years). The one method that comes close is the 30→90-day **term-structure slope difference**: ATM 20-session R²oos +0.283 against the baseline's +0.214 (+0.069, four of five folds, DM p 0.035, not BH-significant among ten); on the secondary same-day label it is +0.404 against +0.319 and BH-significant. Vendor-Greek positioning is worse than the baseline in seven of eight cells across both clocks and never BH-rejected; realized volatility, Lasso and boosting are worse in most cells and never meet the rule (Lasso adds +0.024 on ATM 20-session with four winning folds but DM p 0.49). This is a forecastability result about a measured mid-surface series, not a trade rule, a P&L or an edge.

Contracts with a non-positive vendor IV (about 8% of contracts, deep in the money), with the vendor's error sentinel (`iv_error` 100 with `implied_vol` 0.5 and a zero bid, about 4%) or any residual above 0.01, or with no open interest are excluded and counted. - **The ten methods** (each is the frozen `lagged` features, prior-60 Z and 5-session change, plus the listed columns; linear methods use `StandardizedEstimator(Ridge)`, train-only standardization): (1) multi-scale Z at 20 and 120 sessions plus the 60-session spread sd; (2) each leg's own 60-session Z; (3) smile shape: butterfly spread, half-spread-over-premium spread, and the other surface measure's spread; (4) 1- and 20-session spread changes; (5) term structure: differences of the 30→90 slope, the log expiry-scope OI put/call ratio and the straddle ratio; (6) positioning: differences and 60-session Z of dollar net gamma, log OI-vega, net vanna, log OI put/call and log volume put/call, plus the vega-weighted-IV and size-imbalance differences; (7) realized vol: each leg's 20-session realized vol of adjusted-close log returns and each leg's implied-minus-realized; (8) one pooled Ridge across the three pairs on (1)+(4); (9) `StandardizedEstimator(Lasso)` on the union of (1)–(7); (10) `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` (depth 3, rate 0.05, 20-row leaves) on the same union. Baselines: `mean` and the exact ADR-0272 `lagged` model (raw features, alpha 100 ATM / 0.1 skew).

## Secondary same-day label (reported, outside the family)

On the same-day label (which includes the reversal of the decision day's own surface measurement) the baseline is far stronger (ATM +0.193 / +0.319, skew +0.264 / +0.331) and several methods are BH-rejected: ATM 5-session, methods 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8 (Δ +0.022 to +0.033, four of five folds each); ATM 20-session, methods 2 (+0.021, 3/5) and 5 (**+0.086, 4/5, DM p 0.021**); skew 5-session, methods 4 and 8 (+0.045 / +0.051, 3/5 and 4/5). Method 5 (term structure) is the only addition whose ATM 20-session comparison has a small DM p on both clocks (0.035 and 0.021) with four winning folds on each; Lasso (9) is also positive with four folds on both clocks there (+0.024 and +0.041) but with DM p 0.49 and 0.34. Method 5 is the only candidate this memo would carry to a separate confirmation. Positioning (6) is worse than the baseline in seven of eight cells across both clocks (same-day ATM 5-session +0.006 is the exception) and never BH-rejected; boosting (10) is worse in six of eight and Lasso (9) in five.

## What this does and does not say

- Theta's vendor Greeks, open interest, volume and quote sizes are now read, cleaned and summed correctly (split-safe), and they do not improve the forecast of either pair spread at 5 or 20 sessions with these aggregates and this linear/boosted family. That is a negative result about these features as used, not about the data: contract-level positioning by strike (a gamma profile rather than a net sum), flow (volume signed by side) and intraday timing were not tried.
- The term-structure result is a candidate, not a finding: one of ten, p 0.035 before correction, one bad year, and the same-day confirmation is on a noisier label. A separately registered single-hypothesis check on the untouched later data would be the next step if the owner wants one; re-tuning on 2021–2025 is not.
- Development selection rested on one year (2020) in which the skew folds are mostly one pair; the Ridge alpha grid barely matters for the linear methods (differences of 0.01), but the boosting and Lasso knobs do, and both were chosen on thin evidence.
- No row is imputed; models are compared on exactly the rows every model could score, which removes the split neighbourhoods (the log OI-vega feature is undefined for 60 sessions after the AMZN and GOOGL resets; the AAPL 2020-08-31 reset leaves a finite outlier inside the development fold only) and the days without a 90-day expiry from every comparison.
- The realized-vol leg uses the 20 returns ending at the t−1 close (the panel window is prior-only), one session older than the same-day implied vol it is subtracted from; conservative, and a small handicap for method 7.
- The memo's R²oos is the pair-pooled row-level value (the `all` group of `skill_*.jsonl`); the per-date `skill_vs_mean` block in `skill_*.json` averages across pairs per date and gives slightly different levels (ATM 20-session lagged 0.210, method 5 0.271). DM t and p come from the per-date gap series.
