## Verified correct

- **Hashes and counts.** All 18 pinned SHA-256 values in `run-tech-backtest.json` match; `evaluation.json` is `73c8ccba…c355`; the run log's write hashes match all 13 structural files; the resolved document hash equals the pinned structural config; the Theta fingerprint `7b1d6ab9…` recomputes both through the reader and independently from footers. Memo counts 20,025,208 / 27,242 / 1,830 / 305 / 290 / 15 / 0 and every per-book intent and refusal count equal `evaluation.json`.
- **Sources.** AAPL/AMZN/GOOGL/QQQ: manifest SHA equals bytes, 2,503 common sessions 2016-01-04..2025-12-15 with none missing; `adjusted_close` is continuous across the splits and total-return (QQQ +197.61% matches the memo; price-only +187.16%); dividends sit on the ex-date. Theta: 444 members present and distinct, no missing root-days 2018..2025-12-15, `underlying_price` equals the raw close (median error 0), EOD snapshots (underlying time about 17:15 ET), `sealed_from` refuses any file reaching 2026-01-01. DGS3MO: fingerprint matches; every surface date uses an observation strictly before it (1 or 3 days old).
- **Graph fits.** 7 precision and 7 correlation fits reproduce bit-for-bit from the pinned prices (alpha, CV score, train log-likelihood, iterations, edges, weights, status); the window is the 252 sessions strictly before the fit date; QQQ residualization is an expanding fit inside the window; `cv=TimeSeriesSplit(3)` is forward-chaining, so "chronological CV" is accurate; the signed partial correlation formula is right. Memo fit table and edge frequencies (5/3/4, 7/6/7) match.
- **Z-scores.** All six row files recompute from the prior 60 sessions only (max difference 2e-15, 0 status mismatches); a missing value refuses the signal rather than skipping; pair history is independent of selection; edges come from the latest fit on or before t.
- **Surface.** ATM IV is the mean of call and put mid IVs at the strike nearest the parity forward; risk reversal is put25 minus call25 interpolated in delta without extrapolation; target expiry is the nearest to 30 days within ±7, ties to the earlier; no open-interest field is emitted; both symbols pick the same expiry on all 2,000 dates.
- **Ledger and schedule.** All 610 legs reproduce: sell at bid, buy at ask, $0.65 per contract per side, daily Black-76 hedge with the prior DGS3MO, $0.005 per share, dividends with the right sign on the first hedge day on or after the ex-date (differences at most 2.6e-8). Marks at mid, fees not double counted. Every entry was that day's largest |Z| ≥ 2, filled next session, shorting the higher-Z name; every exit used Z on day x and filled at x+1 with no earlier trigger missed; no overlap within a book; no position spans a split; all 15 hedge-failed exposures are closed with realized P&L in equity.
- **Metrics.** Every total return, Sharpe, information ratio, drawdown, win/closed count, duration, annual cell, ten-block cell and preflight count reconciles; equal-weight +282.23% is daily-rebalanced from the 2019-12-31 close (buy-and-hold would be +263.82%).
- **Tests.** 236 passed across the child suite and the five relevant `tests/pipeline_libs` files. Not covered by tests: that the CV splitter is `TimeSeriesSplit`; that a skew signal trades a skew structure (A1); that the caps permit any trade in a scored year (A2); any use of edge sign; a frozen-data regression of `graph_fits.jsonl`; the refused-exit-then-settled single-leg path.
